# SIH-crop--disease--detection-
#Crop Disease and Pest detection System 
**An AI driven digital solution for the early identification and management of crop diseases to protect the agricultural yields(SIH26131)**
## THE PROBLEM 
Crop diseases and pest infestations causes massive agricultural losses. Traditional manual inspection is slow and often inaccurate, leading to delayed treatment excessive pesticide usage and severe crop damage. Farmers need a fast, highly accessible way to diagnose issues before they spread across the field 
## OUR Solution 
Our project provides a real time diagnostic tool tailored for for the farmers:
* **AI-Powered Image Analysis:** Uses Computer Vision to instantly identify specific diseases from the smartphone or may be form drone images or from satellite data  
* **AI-Powered guidance system so that farmers can access tool easily:** Most of the famers in India are illiterate so the apps has inbuilt guidance system to guide through the app
* **Actionable Advisories:** Provides real time recommendations for targeted chemical or organic treatments
